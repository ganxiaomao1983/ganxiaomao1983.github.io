Before you choose a 401(k) provider

The right plan depends on your team, state, payroll setup, budget, and benefit goals. Before deciding, review current details directly with the provider and ask qualified advisors for guidance where needed.

  • ✓Confirm whether your business and team are eligible for the plan you are considering.
  • ✓Review current employer fees, participant fees, and investment-related fees.
  • ✓Understand any plan setup, contribution, administration, and employer responsibilities.
  • ✓Check how the plan works with your current payroll workflow.
  • ✓Review your state’s retirement-program requirements and available alternatives, if relevant.
  • ✓Ask a qualified tax, legal, benefits, or financial professional about decisions specific to your business.

Frequently asked questions

What is Gusto 401(k)?

Gusto 401(k) is a retirement-plan offering powered by Guideline and designed to work alongside Gusto payroll. Plan structure, features, and eligibility can vary, so it is best to review current plan details directly with Gusto before making a decision.

Do I need to use Gusto payroll?

The offering is designed to integrate with Gusto payroll. Visitors should confirm current availability, requirements, and setup details directly with Gusto, since specifics can change over time.

Can a very small business explore Gusto 401(k)?

Small businesses and self-employed owners may explore retirement-plan options, but suitability and eligibility depend on the business structure, employees, and plan terms. Review current details directly with Gusto to see whether it fits your situation.

Does a 401(k) reduce administrative work?

A payroll-integrated approach may reduce manual coordination between systems. It does not remove all employer administration or compliance responsibilities, which employers should understand and review carefully.

How much does Gusto 401(k) cost?

This page does not list pricing. Fees, plan terms, and availability can change, so review current pricing and disclosures directly with Gusto before deciding.

Can a business receive tax credits for starting a retirement plan?

Some qualifying small businesses may be eligible for federal tax credits, depending on applicable rules and circumstances. Eligibility is not guaranteed. Consult a tax professional and review current official information before assuming any credit applies to your business.
